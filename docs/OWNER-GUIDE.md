# Owner guide

## Signing in

Open the link the commissioner sent. First time: type your email and press **Forgot password / first-time setup**; open the email and choose a password (check spam). Then sign in. You can change your password with the link at the top.

## The clock

Each pick has a time limit (the commissioner sets it; it starts at 1 hour). The clock only runs **11:00 AM to 11:00 PM Eastern**. Overnight it pauses and picks up at 11:00 AM. Your pick goes on the clock the moment the pick before it is made, at any hour, and by default you can make it right then even if the countdown is paused overnight (the commissioner can switch that off). The banner at the top shows who is on the clock and the countdown, and says **It is your pick!** when it is yours.

## Making a pick

1. Open **Players**. Search, filter by position, or sort by clicking a column heading.
2. Under **Drafting for**, choose the pick (your on-clock pick is chosen for you).
3. Press **Draft** next to a player and confirm. You can only pick for a team that currently owns the pick, including picks you received in trades.

## If you miss your clock

Your pick is **skipped** and the next pick goes on the clock. You can still make your skipped pick at any time (it appears in **My picks** as "Skipped"), even while someone else is on the clock. Your future picks cannot be made early: each one unlocks when it comes on the clock (only the commissioner can fill a pick out of turn).

## Draft list and Auto-draft

Press **+ Queue** on players you like, then reorder or remove them on the **Queue** tab (your draft list). Only you and the commissioner can see it. Drafted players drop out automatically. You can also add a **write-in** (a player not in the pool) to the list with his name plus a link or note, checked the same way as a write-in pick.

Turn **Auto-draft** On (checkbox on the Queue tab). Then, when your pick comes up, the site drafts the **first player on your list who is still available**, right away, at any hour. A write-in entry makes a write-in pick marked pending verification; if someone has already written him in, or he is now in the pool, that entry is skipped. With Auto-draft Off, nothing is drafted for you: **Draft top of list** on My picks only runs when you press it.

**If your list runs out:** Auto-draft never picks the best available player. If your pick arrives and nobody on your list is left, nothing is picked, a red **"Your list is exhausted"** message appears at the top of the site and on My picks, and your normal clock keeps running for the full hour. Make a pick yourself before time is up. If the time passes, the pick is skipped like any other and you can still make it later. (An email alert is not built; it is a possible later add-on.)

**When does Auto-draft actually run?** Your list is private: other owners never see it. Auto-draft is carried out by your own open browser, by the commissioner's Monitor page, or by the commissioner's unattended ticker, so it works even if your browser is closed, as long as the commissioner is running the Monitor page or the ticker. If none of those is running when your pick arrives, it happens the next time one of them runs (if your clock has passed by then and the list could have filled it, the list is used before the pick is skipped). If your list has no available player left, nothing is picked, the clock keeps running, and you see a red warning in the app: make a pick before your hour is up. The yellow line **"Clock last checked"** at the top shows when any runner last checked the clock.
## Other tabs

- **Board**: the draft board with the 24 pick slots down the side and the 13 rounds across, the current pick highlighted, then the recent picks. All 24 rows show at once; scroll sideways if your screen cannot fit all rounds.
- **Rosters**: any team's drafted and kept players and remaining picks.
- **History**: all picks so far, plus CSV downloads.

## Player not in the list (write-in)

If a player is not in the pool, open **Players**, expand **Player not in the list? Write him in**, choose the pick, and enter his name plus a link to his page (baseball-reference.com if possible) or a short note that identifies him (for example a high-school player: `HS SS, Tulsa OK, class of 2027`). The pick counts immediately and the clock moves on; the commissioner verifies it afterward and may edit or reject it (a rejected pick comes back to you). If his name is already in the pool you will be asked to pick him from the list, and the same player cannot be written in twice.

The player list also has a **Type** filter: MLB (PUID above 9,999) or Non-MLB (never played in MLB, PUID -1).
